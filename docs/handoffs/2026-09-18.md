## PRINCIPLES

1. **Split "what is true" from "what may be said."** Two files, not one. The first is a factual
   record; the second is a ledger with bands and a framing rule. Most of what is exciting about an
   early venture is an *intention*, and every intention stated as a fact spends credibility that the
   true facts beside it then have to repay.
2. **A primary source is verbatim and is never edited.** Corrections go in the derived ledger, dated
   and sourced. A primary source that gets quietly improved has stopped being one.
3. **Check a source document's date, not only its contents.** Both source PDFs turned out to be
   *pre-event* — one dated six days before the event it describes, one five weeks. Everything in them
   is a plan. The repo had been citing their programmes as records of what happened. `pdfinfo` is two
   seconds and it changed how a third of the facts are banded.
4. **Audit the instructions, not only the output.** The brief itself was found instructing the exact
   failure it existed to prevent — telling the builder to strip a verb that turned an ambition into a
   description of something that exists. No draft had been written yet. The bug was upstream.
5. **An acceptance criterion with no runnable check is a wish.** "A second agent's check exits 0" had
   nothing to run. Now it does, and it is explicitly labelled *necessary, not sufficient* — a string
   matcher cannot judge whether a sentence is true.
6. **Retarget the board row in the same session you retarget the deliverable.** Otherwise the next
   agent to claim it reads instructions that no longer match the repo.
7. **An uncommitted rewrite is invisible to anything reading HEAD — including your own subagents.**
   The cold-read agent was briefed from the stale committed file while the corrected one sat
   uncommitted in the working tree. It reported this itself, which is why the audit was worth running.

  broken artifact; the rule now says re-extract rather than trust it.

  the client, and the client replaced it on being asked. Nobody had ever checked who set it.
- **My own brief.** It instructed the failure mode it was written to prevent, and no draft existed yet
  to reveal it. Principle 4 exists because of this one.
